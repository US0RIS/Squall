# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-26

First release.

- Rain, snow and clear weather in a click-through Metal overlay on every screen.
- Snow piles on visible window top edges and screen bottoms with angle-of-repose sliding and spill-off at window ends.
- Rain splashes, pools, ripples and drips off window corners; rain melts snow; clear weather melts and evaporates.
- Cursor pushes flakes, digs through piles and stirs water; raindrops splash off it; clicks make gusts.
- Piles follow moving windows, fly off on fast drags and hard stops, fall on resize and close, and wait while windows are minimized or on another Space.
- Glass menu-bar panel: weather picker, intensity, wind, behavior switches, launch at login, Shake Off; right-click the icon to cycle weather.
- Idles to zero CPU when the sky is clear and nothing is left on screen.
